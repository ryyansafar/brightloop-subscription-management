## New DataTable props (for the subscriptions builder)
- `sortable: true` on a `Column`: header becomes a link cycling asc, desc, none via `?sort=<key>&dir=asc|desc`; the header gets `aria-sort` and an arrow. Read it in the page with `sortParam(params, ["number", "total"])` (returns `{ sort?, dir }`, only allowed keys pass) and build your `orderBy` from it. Both helpers and `nextSort` are exported from `@/components`.
- `hideBelow: "md" | "lg" | "xl"` on a `Column` (wins over `hideOnMobile`) and `pinRight: true` (sticky right, no wrapping, for action columns).
- `<MobileMeta>` shows a secondary line under a cell on phones, where the matching column is hidden (`below="lg"` keeps it until 1024px).
